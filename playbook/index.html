ai first → ai native_

four questions a board should ask about ai, answered with apohem as proof / by kristofer öhman

thesis
We scale through intelligence, not through effort. Not by replacing people, but by freeing them for the work that needs judgement.
where are we on the way to ai native?

Between cross-functional and transformative. The clearest move: citizen developers building their own tools to replace parts of the SaaS landscape, instead of buying another licence.

how does an idea become something that runs?

One form, one canvas, one gate. Nothing runs unattended until it is production grade.

how much do we let ai do on its own?

Autonomy is earned, not granted. A few hard lines, example data by default, and a killswitch on anything that runs unattended.

how do we stay compliant while moving fast?

Every rule has a home: the policy, every prompt, or the gate. Compliance is built in, not bolted on.

takeaways
  • buildKnow-how, not black boxes. Buy the platforms, build the capability.
  • ownThe business owns what it builds. The centre provides platforms, guardrails and the gate.
  • startFast, then course-correct. A stalled build shows where data and integrations need work.
  • shipHave fun and ship. A few hard lines; everything else is open.
proof
Apohem, a Swedish online pharmacy in the Axel Johnson group, built for regulation: EU AI Act, GDPR, NIS2 and a pharmacy licence. Agents in production, every one behind the same gate. Citizen developers replacing parts of the SaaS landscape. A company-wide AI academy.
